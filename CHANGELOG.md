## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-01

### Added

- Initial release of Agentic AI for Beginners: 36 lessons (00 to 36) plus the capstone, in 11 modules from LLM foundations to production and the company track.
- Every lesson ships as a complete unit: written lesson, runnable code, one exercise, and a worked solution, with a zero-credential demo path.
- Capstone: a business assistant (capture, qualify, book, follow up) with an evaluation suite and tracing required, and a public showcase index for finished learner projects.
- PROGRESS.md learner checklist, curated resources shelf, and a plain-language glossary of 40 plus terms.
- llms.txt course index for AI assistants, translation contribution structure, and the two-tier contribution rule (small fixes by PR, new lessons and translations by issue first).

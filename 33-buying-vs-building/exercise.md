# Exercise 33: Score a real decision

1. **Score your own company** (or a company you know well) in `decision-framework.md`. For every score of 4 or 5, write one sentence of evidence next to it. Scores without evidence are wishes.

2. **Run it.** Add your scores to `score_decision.py` as a third dict and print your verdict. If the verdict surprises you, find the two factors doing the most work and write one paragraph on whether they are really true.

3. **Argue the other side.** Write five sentences making the strongest honest case for the opposite decision. If you cannot, you do not understand your own verdict yet.

Acceptance checks:
- Every 4 or 5 in your worksheet has an evidence sentence.
- Your code runs and validates (try entering a 7: the script should refuse it).
- Your counter-argument paragraph names a real cost or risk, not a strawman.

A worked example, scoring a fictional 30-person home-services company, is in `solution/`.

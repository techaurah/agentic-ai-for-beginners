# Exercise 35: The capstone is the exercise

There is no separate exercise for this lesson. Evaluating and hardening your own capstone is the exercise, graded by the harness, not by an answer key.

**Milestone checklist for this lesson:**

- [ ] Run `python3 eval_harness.py` against the shipped sample and explain, in your project notes, which defect causes each of the five failures.
- [ ] Implement the remaining capstone modules from Lesson 34 (`qualify`, `followup`, `memory_store`, `agent`) if any are still TODO.
- [ ] Copy the harness and scenarios into your project and reach 8/8 against your own `run()`.
- [ ] Add three scenarios of your own, at least one born from a bug you actually hit while building.
- [ ] Complete the hardening pass from the lesson (boundary validation, idempotent writes, decision logging, fail toward a human) and write one paragraph per item in your project README stating what you did.
- [ ] Paste your final harness output into your project README. A reviewer should not have to run anything to see your score.

Do not edit expectations downward to reach green. If you believe a shipped scenario contradicts the Lesson 34 spec, say so in the course Discussions with the spec section quoted; that conversation is also a portfolio artifact.
